01 Getting started

What is PromptCV?

PromptCV is a resume-building product designed to help you create, improve, and tailor a professional resume. Its planned tools bring editing, AI writing support, templates, and job-focused refinement together. AI can suggest; your real experience and your decisions remain at the centre.

How do I get started building a resume?

Start with the information you have—such as your contact details, work or project experience, education, and skills. You can build from a draft, refine the wording, and choose a template. You do not need to have every sentence figured out before you begin.

Do I need an account to use PromptCV?

Creating an account is the planned way to use the PromptCV web app and save resume projects. The public website is separate: you can read its guides and explore product information without using the app.

02 Resumes & CVs

What information should I put on my resume?

Include details relevant to the role: contact information, experience, education, skills, and—where useful—projects or certifications. Choose examples that show what you actually did. You do not have to include every detail from your working life.

Can I edit my resume after creating it?

Editing is central to the planned resume-building experience. You should be able to review and refine your content so the finished resume reflects your real experience and voice. Paid access periods and exact editing availability depend on the final plan details.

What’s the difference between a CV and a resume?

The terms can mean different things depending on the country, industry, and application. In many contexts a resume is a concise, role-focused summary, while a CV may be more comprehensive. Follow the employer’s instructions and local expectations for the opportunity.

03 ATS & optimization

What is an ATS?

An applicant tracking system (ATS) is software some employers use to organize and review job applications. Different employers use different systems and processes, so there is no single format or score that guarantees a particular result.

What makes a resume ATS-friendly?

Clear section headings, readable formatting, and relevant language can make resume information easier to follow. PromptCV is designed to provide ATS guidance, but tools and employer requirements vary and no result is guaranteed.

Can I tailor my resume to a job description?

Job-specific tailoring is part of PromptCV’s planned direction. It can help you notice relevant skills and experience in a job description and consider how to present your own background. Tailoring should clarify a genuine fit—not add qualifications you do not have.

04 AI features

How can AI help with my resume?

PromptCV’s planned AI assistance can help with drafting or improving resume wording, summaries, experience bullets, and skills suggestions. Treat its output as a draft: check it carefully and keep only what accurately represents you.

Do I have the final say on AI suggestions?

Yes. AI should act as an assistant, not an authority on your career. Review, edit, or reject suggestions so your resume stays accurate and sounds like you.

Are there limits on AI use?

Exact AI limits have not been finalized. The One Resume plan document includes provisional usage targets, but they may change after operating costs are validated. Check the pricing page for the current planning notes.

05 Pricing & plans

What is planned for the Free option?

The Free plan is intended to let people try PromptCV and begin a resume. Its planning direction includes one resume project, a basic editor and template, manual editing, preview, saving to an account, limited AI help, and basic ATS guidance. Export availability and final details are still being decided.

What is included in the One Resume plan?

It is planned as one complete resume project—not one AI generation—so you can create, edit, improve, tailor, and export one resume. ₦1,000 is a planning price, subject to cost validation. Suggested usage limits and a 30-day access period are provisional, not final commitments.

Can I create multiple resumes, and what about Pro?

Pro is planned for active job seekers who need multiple resume projects or versions. It is intended to be a subscription, but its price and usage limits have not been set. Broader Career tools are a future direction, not a currently available plan or feature set. See plan details for the latest planning status.

06 Templates

What resume templates are available?

The public templates page showcases seven resume style directions: Modern, Executive, Minimal, Technical, Student, Creative, and Professional. Final availability within the web app may depend on the product plan.

How do I choose a resume template?

Choose a layout that is easy to read and suits the role, industry, and experience you want to present. A template is a frame for your content; clear, relevant information matters more than decorative detail.

Can I customize a template?

PromptCV is planned to let people edit their resume content and choose a template. The exact customization controls available in the web app have not been confirmed yet.

07 Account & privacy

Can I download my resume?

PDF export is part of the planned paid One Resume experience, and export functionality is being considered for Free. Exact availability and limits are still being validated. Check the pricing page for current planning details.

How should I handle personal information?

A resume contains personal information. Share only details needed to build your document, and review the product’s privacy information before creating an account or adding sensitive information. We do not make security or retention promises here that have not been confirmed.

How do I manage or delete my account?

Account-management and data-retention details are still being defined for the web app. For questions about PromptCV, use the contact page; do not send passwords or other sensitive account credentials by email.
