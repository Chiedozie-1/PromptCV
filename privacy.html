YOUR INFORMATION

Privacy Policy

This notice describes the information handled when you join the PromptCV launch waitlist. It does not cover third-party sites or services you visit through links from PromptCV.

Information collected

When you join the waitlist, we collect your email address, your consent to receive launch updates, and the time consent was given. To limit automated abuse, the signup service also uses a keyed, non-reversible hash of the request IP address for rate limiting. Expired hashes are cleaned up when signup requests are processed.

How information is used

Your email address is used to manage the waitlist and send PromptCV launch updates. A signup alert containing your email and signup time may be sent to the PromptCV administrator. The signup form does not send promotional email to subscribers.

Service providers and access

Waitlist records are stored in Supabase. Resend is used to deliver administrator signup alerts when email delivery is configured. These providers process information to provide their services. Subscriber records are restricted to server-side operations and are not publicly readable.

Retention and your choices

We retain your waitlist record while it is needed to manage launch updates. To withdraw consent, unsubscribe, or request deletion, email nictech005@gmail.com from the address you used to sign up. We will update or remove your record in response to your request.

Contact

For questions about this waitlist notice or a request concerning your information, contact nictech005@gmail.com.
