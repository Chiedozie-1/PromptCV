GOOD TO KNOW

Pricing
questions.

Here’s the current plan direction, including what remains subject to validation.

How much does Resu Mate cost?

Resu Mate is planned to have a free option and a One Resume pass with a ₦1,000 one-time planning price. The final One Resume price is subject to cost validation. Pro is planned as a subscription, and Pro and Career prices are still to be determined.

What does One Resume include?

It is intended to cover one complete resume project—not one AI generation—with the ability to create, edit, improve, tailor, and export that resume. The current planning targets are 20 AI actions, 3 job analyses, 3 tailoring runs, 5 PDF exports, and 30 days of access. These targets are provisional and may change after cost validation.

What is planned for Pro and Career?

Pro is intended for active job seekers who need multiple resume projects and versions, job-specific tailoring, and more advanced resume tools. Career is a future direction that may expand into job discovery, application tracking, cover letters, interview preparation, and career support. These capabilities are not guaranteed or currently available.

Are the plan limits and features final?

No. Suggested limits and access periods are planning targets. Exact subscription prices and usage limits will be set after product and operating costs are understood. Resu Mate does not guarantee interviews, job offers, or specific ATS results.
