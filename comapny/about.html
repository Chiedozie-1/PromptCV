WHY PromptCV

You’ve done
the work.
Now what?

A very reasonable question.

You’ve solved problems, learned on the job, helped a team move forward, or found a better way to do something. Then a blank document asks you to explain all of it—briefly, neatly, and in exactly the right words.

That’s the odd little gap a resume has to cross: the distance between doing meaningful work and describing it so someone else can see its value. It can be hard to know what to include, how to phrase it, or how much detail is enough. And when a specific role catches your eye, you may need to tell the story a little differently without making it feel like someone else’s.

PromptCV is being built for that moment. It brings resume creation, writing support, and job-focused refinement into one place, so you can spend less time wrestling with the page and more time deciding what best represents you.

THE TRICKY PART ISN’T YOU

A resume is a small page
with a big job.

It has to make your experience easy to scan, sound like you, and connect with the opportunity in front of you. That’s a lot to ask of a blank page.

01 / FIND THE THREAD ↗

What belongs
in the story?

There’s usually more to your work than a job title. The challenge is spotting the experiences, strengths, and details that help someone understand what you bring.

02 / FIND THE WORDS ✳

How do you say it
with confidence?

Turning everyday responsibilities into clear, specific resume language can take practice. A useful nudge can help you get past the first draft without taking the pen away.

03 / FIND THE FIT ⌁

How does it connect
to this role?

A resume for one opportunity may need a different emphasis from a resume for another. Tailoring should help you draw a relevant connection—not invent one.

↗ The goal isn’t to make every resume sound the same. It’s to make your own experience easier to recognise.

OUR PRODUCT PHILOSOPHY

Helpful by design.
Human at the centre.

AI can be a useful writing partner. It can suggest a clearer phrase, help you organise a thought, or prompt you to add a missing detail. But the person behind the experience should stay in charge of what the resume says.

That belief shapes the way PromptCV is planned: not as a career authority handing down answers, but as a practical set of tools that helps you move from rough notes to a resume you can stand behind.
