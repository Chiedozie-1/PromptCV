What is PromptCV?

PromptCV is an AI-powered resume platform designed to help you create, improve, and tailor a professional resume. AI offers suggestions; you stay in control of your story.

What does ATS-friendly mean?

Applicant tracking systems help employers organize applications. Clear formatting and relevant language can make resume details easier to read, but no resume can guarantee a particular screening result.

Can I tailor my resume to a job?

PromptCV is designed to help you compare your experience with a job description and identify relevant skills and areas to refine.

Do I have the final say on AI suggestions?

Yes. AI is a writing assistant, not an authority on your career. Review and edit suggestions so your resume reflects your real experience and voice.
